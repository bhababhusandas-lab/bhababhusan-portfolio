BENCH 1

Hardware bench.

EXH. 01 · MECHANICAL

Four-stroke engine

Intake, compression, power, exhaust: two turns of the crank for every bang. The valves open only on their stroke.

EXH. 02 · MECHANICAL

Planetary gearbox

Sun 12T drives three 18T planets inside a fixed 48T ring. The carrier comes out 5× slower and 5× stronger.

TAP TO REVERSE

EXH. 03 · ELECTRICAL

Three-phase supply

Three phasors 120° apart trace the R, Y and B waves. At every instant they add up to zero, which is why a balanced load needs no neutral current.

EXH. 04 · MECHANICAL

Centrifugal governor

Spin faster and the flyballs swing out, lifting the sleeve that closes the steam valve. This was feedback control before electronics.

EXH. 05 · ELECTRICAL

Lissajous scope

Feed one sine to X and another to Y. The frequency ratio draws the shape, and a slow phase drift makes it turn.

TAP THE SCREEN TO CHANGE THE RATIO

EXH. 06 · MECHANICAL

Cam & follower

A pear-shaped cam turns rotation into a timed lift, the same trick that opens the valves in Exhibit 01.

EXH. 07 · DIGITAL

4-bit counter

Each clock edge ripples through four flip-flops. Same number, three ways: binary LEDs, decimal and hex.

TAP TO COUNT UP / DOWN

EXH. 08 · ELECTRICAL

RC charging

A square wave pushes charge into a capacitor through a resistor. Small τ follows the edges, big τ smooths them into a ripple.

EXH. 09 · MECHANICAL

Four-bar linkage

Four bars, four pins, one motor. A point on the coupler draws this teardrop, the basis of wipers, pumps and walking machines.

EXH. 10 · PHYSICS

Pendulum wave

Fifteen pendulums, each a little shorter than the last, seen from above. They drift into snakes and braids, then line up again every 24 seconds.

EXH. 11 · AI

Neural network

A tiny 3-5-5-2 network doing a forward pass. Blue weights excite, red ones inhibit, and softmax turns the last layer into a guess.

EXH. 12 · AI

Training run

Twenty epochs in a terminal. The loss drops fast and then flattens: most of the learning happens early.

EXH. 13 · ALGORITHMS

Sorting race

Same shuffled list, same speed per step. Quicksort finishes while bubble sort is still swapping neighbours.

TAP TO SHUFFLE

EXH. 14 · ALGORITHMS

A* pathfinding

A* explores toward the goal, guided by distance, then traces the shortest route back. This is how maps and game characters find their way.

TAP FOR A NEW MAZE

EXH. 15 · WEB

Request lifecycle

Client to API to database and back. A cache answers many requests without touching the database, and much faster.

EXH. 16 · DATA STRUCTURES

Hash map

Each key is hashed to a bucket number. When two keys land in the same bucket they chain together, which is why lookups stay fast but not free.

EXH. 17 · ALGORITHMS

Binary search

On a sorted list, check the middle and throw half away. Sixteen items take at most four looks.
